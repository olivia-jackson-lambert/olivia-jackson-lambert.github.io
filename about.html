About

Hi! I’m Olivia Jackson Lambert. I lead the data team at Hockeystick, where I’m responsible for the platform, the analytics, and the models other teams build on.

I came to leadership through building — physics first, then consulting, then campaign data at national scale — and I still think the interesting part of this work is the decision at the end of it, not the model in the middle.

When I’m not working, I’m usually kayaking, reading, making art, or hanging out with my husband and three cats.

How I Work

01

Start from the decision

A model is only worth what it changes. Before building, I want to know what someone will actually do differently once the number exists — and if the answer is nothing, that's worth finding out early.

02

Name the trade-off

Every model gives something up. I'd rather say out loud what a choice costs — accuracy traded away to clear a safety threshold, say — than hand over a single score as though it came free.

03

Build for people who shouldn't have to ask

The measure of a data team isn't how fast it answers questions, it's how rarely anyone needs to ask. Self-serve tooling beats a shorter ticket queue every time.

04

Own the whole path

Cost, uptime and the state of the pipeline are part of the modelling work, not somebody else's problem downstream. A model nobody can run on a Tuesday morning isn't finished.
