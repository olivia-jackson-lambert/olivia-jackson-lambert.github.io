Values

How I lead

Be Proactive

I look for the answer before asking for it, in the documentation, the tools or a colleague, because independent research makes for stronger solutions and sharper questions.

Take Ownership

Not every decision needs sign-off. When someone sees what needs doing and has the knowledge to do it, I want them to act with confidence.

Iterate on Imperfection

Progress over perfection. Three early drafts shaped by stakeholders beat one polished deliverable that arrives too late.

Challenge Assumptions

The way something has always been done is not a reason to keep doing it. Good ideas come from every level of a team, and disagreement is welcome.

Understand Our Users

A data team is infrastructure for everyone else. Knowing how non-technical colleagues use data is what makes the tooling worth building.

About

Physicist by training

A data leader with a physicist's habits: careful with measurement, wary of any single number, and most interested in the decision a model is meant to serve.

My last two roles have been in political and civic data, first at the Democratic National Committee and now leading data at Hockeystick, where the work connects campaigns and organisations with the people who support them. It is the work I want to keep doing.

I trained in astrophysics at the University of Edinburgh, and my research on sampling for Mars missions was published in Nature Astronomy. I am now completing a Master's in Data Science at UC Berkeley.

Outside work I am usually kayaking, reading, making art, or at home with my husband and our three cats.
